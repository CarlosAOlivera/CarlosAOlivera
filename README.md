### 🎓 ELEVATE

A modern ESL learning platform built from the perspective of a classroom teacher.

ELEVATE brings assignments, learning resources, student workflows, and AI-assisted tutoring into a unified environment designed around real classroom use.

At its core is **Eli**, an AI-powered Socratic ESL tutor that helps students with vocabulary, grammar, reading comprehension, and writing while encouraging them to develop their own answers rather than simply generating the work for them.

ELEVATE is also where I experiment with an important question:

> **How can AI support students without replacing the thinking and learning process?**

`ESL` · `EdTech` · `AI Tutoring` · `Student Experience` · `Teacher Tools`

### 📡 MeshHubPR

A Spanish-language knowledge and experimentation hub focused on **Meshtastic, LoRa, and resilient communications**.

MeshHubPR combines practical education, documentation, tools, and real-world field testing to make mesh communication technology more accessible to Spanish-speaking communities.

Areas include:

`Learning Center` · `Hardware` · `Tutorials` · `Coverage Maps` · `MeshHub Labs` · `Tools` · `Community`

**Current stack**

`Next.js` · `React` · `TypeScript` · `Tailwind CSS` · `Markdown`
